Turn your photo and a caption into multi-panel poster cover art in the style
of a Vice City sunset — right in the browser, fully offline. See the
competition at [make.fun/c/gta6-cover-art-01](https://make.fun/c/gta6-cover-art-01).

## What it does

- **Your photos, on your machine.** Upload photos from your own files, or tap
  a sample photo to try it instantly. Photos are read locally and never
  uploaded anywhere.
- **Multi-panel poster layouts.** Hero + strip (one big panel over three
  small ones), Grid 2×2, or a full-bleed single shot.
- **Crop & position per panel.** Click a panel to select it, drag its photo on
  the preview to move it, use the slider to zoom. One photo can fill every
  panel — or each panel can hold a different photo.
- **Captions & grades.** A big display title plus a glowing script
  sub-caption, in three colour grades: Sunset, Neon Night and Golden Hour.
  Film grain, a vignette and thin poster-board frames finish the look.
- **Export PNG in three post sizes.** 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080,
  one click each.

## Run it

Serve `app/` with any static server (for example `node serve.ts`, then open
http://localhost:8080) — no build step, no network requests. Fonts (Anton and
Pacifico, SIL Open Font License) and sample photos live inside `app/`.

An original design: no Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts.
